VDJtools, AIRR and MiXCR tables, plain or gzipped. The format is read from the file's column header, so there is nothing to choose; a header that matches none of the three is rejected rather than guessed at. Leave Chain on Guess to derive it from the data, which also splits a file holding both chains into two samples.

Filter your samples before uploading: remove sequencing noise and the naive repertoire so that only expanded clonotypes and enriched meta-clonotypes remain. VDJtools does this.
