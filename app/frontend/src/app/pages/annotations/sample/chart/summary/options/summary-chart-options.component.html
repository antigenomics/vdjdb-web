P-value is a Beta-Binomial upper tail. A healthy control repertoire of the same species and chain is searched against VDJdb through the same filters as this sample, and the question asked is: of the clonotypes here that matched VDJdb at all, did more of them land on this epitope than the control's matches did. Conditioning on having matched is what makes the two comparable — repertoires differ several-fold in how much of them VDJdb contains at all, for reasons of sequencing depth rather than of antigen. The control rate carries a ½-count prior, so an epitope the control never reached is treated as rare rather than impossible. It counts clonotypes only, so neither the read-count weighting nor the axis scaling changes it. P-adj is Benjamini-Hochberg across every epitope tested in the sample.
